## FortiGate VPN on Mac

- **FortiGate SSL-VPN on Mac:** FortiGate SSL-VPNs connect again on macOS. Since 0.1.44, many stopped with a timeout right after the login
- **Updates on Mac:** North running straight from the downloaded image (not moved to Applications) is now recognized as such, and two update checks at the same time no longer get in each other's way

See the [VPNs](vpns) chapter in the manual.
