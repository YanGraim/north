## VPNs

- **VPNs from North's icon:** turn any VPN on or off from the icon in the menu bar (macOS) or in the tray next to the clock (Windows and Linux), without opening the window
- **Closing the window keeps the VPN up:** with VPNs set up, closing the window leaves North in its icon with the VPNs on; to really quit, use **Sair do North** in the icon
- **FortiGate IPsec on Windows:** Windows itself now reaches the VPN's servers, not only WSL

See the [VPNs](vpns) chapter in the manual.
