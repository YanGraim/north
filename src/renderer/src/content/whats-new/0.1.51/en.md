## Organizations: databases, logins and VPN for the team

- **Databases and logins in the organization:** databases and web portals now live in the organization, like connections. You can share a single access, and a Viewer connects without seeing the password
- **The VPN comes up by itself for everyone:** the organization keeps only the VPN gateway. Each person signs in with their own username and password, and opening the SSH, the database or the portal brings the right VPN up
- **Copy from My workspace:** in Settings → Inventory, inside the organization, pick several clients and bring them all at once, with connections, databases, logins, tags and VPN
- **CSV with databases and logins:** importing inside the organization now brings databases and logins too

See the [VPNs](vpns) chapter in the manual.
