## Release notes and SSH keys in organizations

- **Release notes:** open an update in **Últimas atualizações** and click **Notas da versão**. North drafts the notes from the commits (new, improvement and fix, grouped by scope and with the ticket code); adjust the texts and copy the image into WhatsApp or Teams, or save it as a PNG
- **Git tag on the update:** when the updated commit has a tag (with or without `v`), it shows next to the commit and fills in the version of the notes
- **SSH key in organizations:** the key path (**Caminho da chave**) of organization connections is now kept in your North. It used to get lost and key-based SSH failed to connect. Fill in the path once more in each connection

See the [Workflows](workflows) chapter in the manual.
