## Organizations: share only what each person needs

- **A single connection:** give access to one server, with its saved password. The person sees only that connection and, as a Viewer, connects without ever seeing or copying the password
- **Teams:** share projects with a team and everyone in it gets access. Whoever leaves the team loses it right away
- **Teams from Entra:** link a team to a Microsoft Entra group and it keeps itself up to date at each sign-in
- **New admin panel:** overview, a page per person with everything they can reach, teams, the tree down to each connection and an audit with filters

See the [Overview](overview) chapter in the manual.
